# YatraSetu Database Schemas

This document lists the Mongoose database schemas registered for YatraSetu's 16 collections.

---

## 1. Mapped Models

1. **User**: Login credentials and role attributes (`TOURIST`, `VENDOR`, `ADMIN`).
2. **Vendor**: Business profiles, verification status, and registered GPS coordinates.
3. **Destination**: Coordinates, state heritage parameters, entry fees, and travel seasons.
4. **Hotel**: Verified homestays and accommodation units.
5. **Restaurant**: Verified local eateries and average meal costs.
6. **Guide**: Availability calendars, experience, and pricing details.
7. **Transport**: Seats, pricing, departures, and travel modes.
8. **Verification**: Uploaded licensing document logs.
9. **Review**: Rating text, reviewer references, and sentiment flags.
10. **TrustScore**: Clear 0-100 breakdown records (docs, video walkthrough, reviews).
11. **Trip**: Budget goals, travelers, and dates.
12. **Itinerary**: Day-by-day transport, stay, guide, and dining plans.
13. **Booking**: Customer service bookings.
14. **Payment**: Amount, transaction status, and payment logs.
15. **Complaint**: Active tourist feedback and vendor responses.
16. **Notification**: User dashboard alert logs.
